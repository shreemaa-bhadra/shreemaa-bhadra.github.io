Few curries bring as much warmth, aroma, and comforting restaurant-style richness to the dining table as a freshly prepared **Kadhai Chicken** (or *Kadai Chicken*). The soul of this iconic North Indian dish lies in its signature freshly roasted and ground spice blend an irresistible melange of coriander seeds, cumin, fennel, whole peppercorns, cinnamon, bay leaf, and dry red chillies that immediately fills your home with the most welcoming aroma.

What makes this recipe extra special is the interplay of textures and rich, balanced flavors. Tender, succulent cubes of chicken thighs simmer in a spiced onion-tomato masala enriched with creamy cashew paste, while quick sautéed bell peppers and sweet onion petals maintain their delightful crunch. Finished with a gentle rub of fragrant kasuri methi, every bite is wonderfully savory, mildly spiced, and deeply satisfying.

<!--more-->

Whether you are cooking a festive weekend dinner for family or looking for a nourishing, comforting weeknight curry, this recipe comes together seamlessly in one pot. The velvety cashew paste softens the heat of the chillies and peppercorns, yielding a luscious sauce that coats warm rotis and fluffy rice like a dream.

## Ingredients for Kadhai Chicken:

### 1. Freshly Ground Kadhai Masala:
1. Bay leaf - 1 piece
2. Cinnamon stick - 2 inch piece
3. Black peppercorns - 1 tsp
4. Whole coriander seeds - 2 tsp
5. Cumin seeds - 1 tsp
6. Fennel seeds (saunf) - 1 tsp
7. Dry whole red chillies - 5 pieces

### 2. Sautéed Veggies:
8. Avocado oil or ghee - 1 tsp
9. Green bell pepper - 1 large, diced into 1-inch squares
10. Onion - 1 medium, cut into 1-inch squares and petals separated

### 3. Chicken Gravy Base & Finish:
11. Avocado oil or ghee - 1 tbsp
12. Onion - 1 large, finely chopped
13. Ginger-garlic paste - 1 tbsp
14. Tomato paste/puree - made from 2 fresh tomatoes
15. Chicken thighs (boneless, skinless) - 2 lbs, cut into 1-inch cubes
16. Salt - according to taste (approx. 1 tsp)
17. Cashew paste - 9 to 10 cashews soaked in warm water and blended until smooth
18. Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves) - 1 tbsp, lightly crushed between palms

## Steps of Preparation:

Preparation Time: 20 mins | Cooking Time: 35 mins

- **Roast and grind the Kadhai Masala:** In a small dry skillet over medium-low heat, add the bay leaf, cinnamon stick, black peppercorns, coriander seeds, cumin seeds, fennel seeds, and dry red chillies. Roast gently for 2–3 minutes until deeply aromatic and lightly toasted (be careful not to burn them). Turn off the heat, let the spices cool completely, and grind them in a spice grinder into a coarse-fine powder. Set aside.

- **Sauté the bell peppers and onions:** Heat 1 tsp of avocado oil or ghee in a pan over high heat. Add the squared green bell peppers and onion petals. Sauté briskly for 2 minutes until slightly blistered around the edges while still crisp and crunchy. Transfer to a plate and keep aside.

- **Build the aromatic gravy base:** In a wide, heavy-bottomed pot or traditional kadhai, heat 1 tbsp of avocado oil or ghee over medium heat. Add the finely chopped onions and sauté until they turn golden brown. Add the ginger-garlic paste and sauté for another 1–2 minutes until the raw aroma completely disappears.

- **Cook the tomato puree:** Pour in the pureed fresh tomatoes and add salt to taste. Cook the mixture over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes are well cooked and tiny droplets of oil begin to ooze out from the edges of the masala.

- **Cook the chicken thighs:** Add the 1-inch cubed chicken thighs to the pot. Stir well to coat the chicken with the onion-tomato base. Cover with a lid and cook on medium heat for about 10–12 minutes, allowing the chicken to release its natural juices and cook thoroughly until tender.

- **Add Kadhai Masala and Cashew Paste:** Once the chicken is cooked through, stir in the freshly ground kadhai masala. Cook for 1–2 minutes to allow the spices to meld into the sauce. Pour in the smooth cashew paste and mix thoroughly. Let the curry simmer on low-medium heat for 3–4 minutes until the gravy turns rich, velvety, and oil lightly separates along the surface.

- **Add crunchy veggies & garnish:** Fold in the reserved sautéed green bell peppers and onions. Gently rub the kasuri methi between your palms and sprinkle it over the curry. Give everything a gentle stir, simmer for 1 more minute, and remove from heat.

## Serving Suggestions:

- Serve piping hot with freshly puffed chapatis, tawa rotis, or garlic naan.
- Pairs wonderfully with fragrant [Jeera Rice]({% post_url 2015-05-20-jeera-rice %}) or steamed basmati rice.
- Accompany with crisp cucumber slices, lemon wedges, and a fresh coriander-mint chutney.
